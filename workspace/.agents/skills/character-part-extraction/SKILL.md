name: character-part-extraction
description: Turn a posed anime character reference into a reviewed front A-pose and isolated semantic part images for independent mesh generation. Use for character image normalization, 拆件, and 部件图 generation before modeling.
---

1. Inspect the supplied image. Use image editing with that image as reference to generate a complete front A-pose. Match face, palette, hair silhouette, clothing layers and footwear. Keep hands clear of the torso and feet on a common ground line. Move handheld props beside the character with a visible gap. Save and visually inspect the full-resolution pose before extraction. Correct clipped anatomy or identity drift at this step.
2. Use the saved A-pose as the source for every extraction. Plan ownership from the actual outfit. Assign the entire neck to the hairless head with ears; end the headless body at the base-of-neck seam. Match exposed skin color, shading and linework on the body to the A-pose face and hands. Use simple opaque base garments where coverage is needed. Reconstruct obscured hair volume, locks, garment panels, inner surfaces and hems from visible cues. Record any unobserved shapes as inferred. Keep coupled upper-garment elements (sleeves, cuffs, lapels, integrated layers, trim) together as one upper garment; when outer and inner garments obscure each other and define one silhouette, group the entire layered costume as one upper-outfit guide, including any continuous skirt. Preserve their overlap. Assign hair ornaments to hair and skirt trim to its skirt. Group paired shoes and socks when appropriate. Preserve continuous dresses. Split independent handheld props. Record ambiguities and inferred hidden surfaces.
3. Generate one front guide per planned part, individually or in a labeled-by-position atlas. For an atlas, use a known grid with gutters, check actual cell boundaries, and save a separate PNG for each occupied cell. Preserve the full A-pose as scale reference when cells use independent framing.
4. Compare each image with the A-pose. Inspect hair completeness in front and rear views: where the face or torso occluded a long rear curtain, show its inward-facing strands behind the fringe; white background is appropriate only along genuinely unobstructed sight lines. Verify head exclusion, bald head, neck boundary, sleeve and waist ownership, footwear pairing, colors, missing or duplicated accessories, and clipping. Correct failed parts using the saved A-pose. Record source and A-pose hashes, part file paths, ownership, inferred details and review status in a case manifest. Show the pose and parts before advancing to meshes if the user requests a review.
